Once upon a time, in a small village near the forest, there was a kind hearted old lady who lived in a

small house. The old woman loved her garden and one flower she always loved is tulip. Because of it

she built a small full with colorful tulip.

Because of her love too, she made a small bed in the middle of the tulip garden and often spent the

night there. One night, she slept in the middle of the garden. But there was something strange because

unlike usual, she heard a sound from the garden. There was babies laughing and sweet singing. She

tried to find out where the sound came from but she found nothing

On the following night, she once again slept in the garden. And once again, she heard the sounds: babie

laughing and sweet singing. She was curious. She walked along the garden quietly. She was surprised:

there, somewhere in the garden, she found a little fairy mother rocking and crooning the tulip flower

like a cradle. There was a little fairy baby, laughing and playing the tulip cup.

The old woman walked back to her house. From that moment, she never picked the tulip. She did not

allow other people touch her beloved flowers ever.
